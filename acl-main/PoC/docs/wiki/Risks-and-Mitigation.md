# Risks and Mitigation

This page lists the risks that came out of this PoC. Likelihood and impact are marked **(inference)**: the PoC did not measure them. Every mitigation is a `[RECOMMENDATION]` and is not implemented.

| ID | Risk | Origin | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation strategy | Evidence/Status |
|----|------|--------|------------|--------|---------------------|-----------------|
| R-01 | The ACL matches a contract that is not the monolith's real one, so a future integration breaks. | Finding F-1; assumption A-01; unknown U-01; limitation "contract not confirmed". | Medium **(inference)** | High **(inference)** | `[RECOMMENDATION]` Confirm the real contract and the assumptions A-01…A-10 with the monolith owners before the real ACL. | Open. The contract used is a team proposal (`CONTRATO_OCR.md` lines 1-4). |
| R-02 | The simulated behaviour is mistaken for real OCR behaviour (quality, confidence or text treated as meaningful). | Limitation "OCR is simulated"; A-07; decision D-20. | Medium **(inference)** | Medium **(inference)** | `[RECOMMENDATION]` Keep the engine behind `IOcrEngine` and the `EngineDescriptor` marker until the real engine replaces it. | Mitigated in the PoC: TC-UNIT-011 and TC-ISO-001…TC-ISO-005 prove the marker and the simulation are confined. |
| R-03 | Coupling is re-introduced through shared types (a layer leaks its types into another, or the engine type is used outside `Infrastructure`). | Decision D-35; architecture checks. | Low **(inference)** | High **(inference)** | `[RECOMMENDATION]` Keep the architecture tests (TC-ARC-001…TC-ARC-005) in CI for the real ACL. | Mitigated in the PoC: TC-ARC-001…TC-ARC-005 green in `run-02.trx`. |
| R-04 | An error leaks internal detail (stack trace, exception name, namespace) in production. | Findings F-3; decisions D-11, D-12, D-13, D-18; CT-18. | Low **(inference)** | High **(inference)** | `[RECOMMENDATION]` Keep the single error-handling component registered first and run the isolation tests in every environment. | Mitigated in the PoC: TC-ERR-002 (Production) and TC-ISO-003 green in `run-02.trx`. |
| R-05 | A missing or wrong `Content-Type` is mishandled, so a valid request fails or an invalid one passes. | Defect TC-INT-006 (Phase 4); A-06; D-08. | Low **(inference)** | Medium **(inference)** | `[RECOMMENDATION]` Keep the explicit content-type check and its tests (TC-INT-005, TC-INT-006). | Fixed and mitigated in the PoC: TC-INT-005, TC-INT-006 green in `run-02.trx`. |
| R-06 | A wrong-type field returns an error without the failing field name, so the monolith cannot react. | Defect TC-VAL-010…TC-VAL-014 (Phase 4); A-04; D-17. | Low **(inference)** | Medium **(inference)** | `[RECOMMENDATION]` Keep the `JsonException.Path`-based field derivation and its tests. | Fixed and mitigated in the PoC: TC-VAL-010…TC-VAL-014 green in `run-02.trx`. |
| R-07 | A large payload exhausts memory because the whole document is decoded at once. | Assumption A-10; limitation "no size limit". | Medium **(inference)** | Medium **(inference)** | `[RECOMMENDATION]` Decide a size limit or a streaming path before the real ACL. | Open. Not measured by the PoC. |
| R-08 | The real engine changes the contract, breaking the monolith. | Unknown U-03. | Medium **(inference)** | High **(inference)** | `[RECOMMENDATION]` Fix the ACL-to-engine contract as a port and test it before swapping the engine. | Open. The engine does not exist yet. |
